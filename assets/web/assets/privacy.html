Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026 · Bright Future Technologies LLC ("BFT", "we")

This policy explains what information BFT Services (the dispatch platform at demo.brightfuturetechllc.com and its apps) and this website collect, why, and how it is protected. BFT Services is used by businesses ("customers") to run their own operations, so much of the information in it belongs to and is controlled by that customer.

Information we collect

  • Website requests. When you fill in "Request a walkthrough" we store your name, company, email, phone, interest and message so we can contact you.
  • Account information. Company name and an owner contact (name, email, phone). Staff and drivers sign in with access codes and PINs; we store only a one-way hash of each code, never the code itself.
  • Operational data entered by customers. Tow calls, freight loads, addresses, vehicle and plate details, customer names and phone numbers, notes, photos, weigh-station results and job timelines.
  • Driver location. When a driver taps "Share location" in the driver app, the device's position is sent to update the dispatcher map and ETAs. Location is only sent while the app is open and sharing is on.
  • Technical data. IP addresses and request logs used for security, rate limiting and troubleshooting.

How we use it

To provide and secure the service, show dispatchers and drivers the jobs they work on, produce the customer's reports and exports, respond to requests, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information and we do not use customer operational data for advertising.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Platform data is hosted in Microsoft Azure data centers in the United States, encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Secrets are kept in Azure Key Vault.
  • Maps use OpenStreetMap; address lookups use the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service, which receives the address being looked up.
  • Turn-by-turn directions open in the driver's own maps app (for example Google Maps) when the driver chooses "Navigate".
  • If a customer exports data (CSV or a linked Google Sheet), that copy is controlled by the customer.

Data separation

Each customer company's data is kept in its own logically separate workspace. Users of one company cannot see another company's jobs, drivers or customers.

Retention

Customer data is kept while the account is active or paused. When a customer asks us to close their account, we delete their workspace data within 30 days, except where we must keep records for legal reasons. Website requests are kept until they are no longer needed for follow-up. The public demo workspace contains fictional data and is reset regularly.

Cookies and storage

The platform stores your sign-in session in your browser's session storage (cleared when the tab closes) and a few display preferences in local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

If your information was entered by a business that uses BFT Services, please contact that business first. You can also contact us to access, correct or delete information we hold about you.

Health information

The Health Triage module is a demonstration with fictional data only. Do not enter real patient information.

Children

BFT Services is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.
